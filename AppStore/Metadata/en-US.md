## Description

ScreenFree is a native macOS screen recorder and non-destructive video editor for polished product demos, tutorials, and workflow videos.

Flexible recording
• Capture a display, a window, or an area you draw with the pointer
• Record system audio and microphone as independent sources
• Add an optional camera overlay, countdown, pause, and resume

Automatic focus
• Create cursor-following zooms from clicks and explanation gestures
• Use natural easing for zooming, panning, and recovery
• Move, trim, and tune every zoom range on the timeline

Precise editing
• Split, trim, remove, merge, change speed, and adjust volume
• Find speech and silence with a decoded audio waveform
• Add captions, click effects, spotlight highlights, and shortcut labels
• Undo changes, magnify the timeline, and preview full screen

Professional output
• Choose 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, or custom dimensions
• Tune backgrounds, padding, corners, shadows, and camera layout
• Export H.264/AAC MP4 or looping GIF
• Select 720p, 1080p, 4K, custom resolution, and multiple frame rates

ScreenFree processes recording and editing content locally on your Mac. It does not upload your recordings, audio, cursor path, or captions to ScreenFree servers.
