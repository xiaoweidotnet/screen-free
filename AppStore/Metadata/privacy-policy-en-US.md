# ScreenFree Privacy Policy

Effective date: `[ENTER DATE]`

ScreenFree respects your privacy. This policy explains how the ScreenFree macOS app handles information.

## Local processing

ScreenFree records and processes the screen, window, or area that you explicitly choose. Depending on your settings, the content may include system audio, microphone audio, camera video, cursor positions, click events, and keyboard shortcuts.

This content is used to provide recording, editing, automatic zoom, captioning, and export features. It is stored on your device or at a location you choose. ScreenFree does not upload this content to ScreenFree servers.

## System permissions

ScreenFree may request Screen Recording, Microphone, Camera, Input Monitoring, and Speech Recognition permissions. You can revoke these permissions at any time in macOS System Settings. Revoking a permission disables the feature that depends on it without affecting unrelated features.

## Collection and tracking

The current version has no account system, advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or remote crash collection. ScreenFree does not sell personal information.

## Your control

You can manage or delete recordings, project files, and exports in Finder. Removing the app does not automatically delete files that you saved to other folders.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and a new effective date will be shown.
