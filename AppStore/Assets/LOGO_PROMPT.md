# Logo 生成记录

- 生成方式：Codex 内置 `image_gen` 工具
- 用途分类：`logo-brand`
- 原始输出：1254×1254 PNG、RGB、无 Alpha
- App Store 主图：从同一主图缩放为 1024×1024，全部小尺寸图标均由该主图确定性派生

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: master app icon for a native macOS screen recorder and video editor named ScreenFree
Primary request: create an original, memorable app icon mark that combines the idea of a screen canvas with cursor-focused zoom and video editing; the mark should communicate screen recording, precise focus, and polished motion
Subject: one bold centered symbol: a clean screen/frame shape integrated with a focused cursor or magnification motif, using balanced negative space; avoid tiny controls or literal UI screenshots
Style/medium: premium vector-like app icon, minimal geometric construction, crisp edges, flat-to-subtle dimensional color, strong silhouette, professional macOS utility aesthetic
Composition/framing: exact square 1024x1024 canvas, full-bleed square background layer, primary mark centered with generous safe margin, readable at 16px; do not pre-mask or pre-round the outer canvas corners because the operating system applies the final mask
Color palette: deep indigo and vivid electric violet with a restrained cyan accent; bright off-white symbol for high contrast
Lighting/mood: confident, creative, precise, modern; subtle depth only, no photorealism
Constraints: no text, no letters, no watermark, no mockup, no device frame, no screenshot, no trademarked shapes, no Apple logo, no transparency, no ornamental clutter; one icon only; original design; retain clear high-contrast silhouette at small sizes
Avoid: camera lens cliché, play button cliché, film clapperboard cliché, excessive gradients, glass reflections, drop-shadow haze, multiple separate symbols
```
